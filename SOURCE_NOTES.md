# Initial Official Source Notes

These notes are a starting point only. They must be revalidated before production.

Official NSFDC FAQ currently states:
- NSFDC is a PSU under the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment.
- Current annual family income ceiling for concessional loans is ₹5 lakh, subject to amendment.
- Loan applications are routed through authorized State Channelizing Agencies/other Channelizing Agencies; direct applications to NSFDC are not entertained.
- The FAQ describes multiple scheme categories including Micro Finance Scheme, Aajeevika Micro-Finance Yojana, Term Loan, Udyam Nidhi Yojana and Educational Loan Scheme.
- It also describes multiple channel-partner categories and partner prudential norms.

Important: the official site contains policy/lending documents whose details may differ by policy version, channel partner or effective date. Therefore, the application must use a versioned scheme configuration with source metadata rather than hard-coding values from this note.
