# Content & Localization Guidelines

## Audience
People who may have limited digital literacy, limited financial literacy, intermittent internet access, or limited familiarity with government terminology.

## Writing rules
Use:
- "loan amount" instead of "credit exposure"
- "your own contribution" instead of "margin money" unless the official term is required
- "authorized agency/partner" and explain it once
- short sentences
- bullets
- examples

Avoid:
- legalistic paragraphs
- unexplained acronyms
- English-only financial jargon
- claims such as "guaranteed loan"

## Hindi
Use natural, easy Hindi. Do not produce literal machine translations that become difficult to understand.

Example:
"यह वेबसाइट केवल जानकारी और अनुमान देने में मदद करती है। अंतिम पात्रता और ऋण स्वीकृति संबंधित अधिकृत एजेंसी तय करेगी।"

## Bengali and other languages
Translation keys must be content-managed and versioned. Do not embed translated strings inside business logic.

## Financial content
Always display:
- amount
- rate
- tenure
- source
- source checked date
- disclaimer where relevant

## Official-source links
Use deep links to the relevant official NSFDC page/document where possible. Never disguise an unofficial source as official.

## Trust statement
Recommended:
"योजना की जानकारी NSFDC की आधिकारिक जानकारी के आधार पर दी गई है। नियम बदल सकते हैं। आवेदन और अंतिम पात्रता के लिए अधिकृत एजेंसी से पुष्टि करें।"
